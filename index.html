Welcome to my website!

Hello! I’m TJ Miranda—a U.S. Army Veteran, U.S. Navy spouse, and dedicated public servant currently working for the U.S. Navy as a federal civilian Utility Systems Operator (Water & Wastewater Treatment Specialist). My career spans military service, public utilities, leadership, and hands-on technical work.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and am currently back in college to refresh and sharpen my core technical skills. Long before modern web development, I was deeply immersed in tech as one of only two hackers in Oriental Mindoro during the 1990s. Beyond my technical background, I have spent years serving my local community in Kings County, assisting local residents with immigration processes and legal paperwork.
